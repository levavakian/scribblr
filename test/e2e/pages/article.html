The cow is a large domesticated mammal, raised on farms around the world for milk, meat and leather.

Despite their calm reputation, cows can run surprisingly fast when something frightens them in the field.

In many cultures, cows hold a special place in stories, festivals and everyday family life.

Young cows are called calves, and they usually stand up within an hour of being born.

A cow has a single stomach with four compartments, which lets it digest tough plants slowly.

Cows communicate with each other using a wide range of sounds, from soft grunts to loud calls.

Most cows sleep for only about four hours a day, often in short naps while lying down.

The oldest known cow lived to nearly fifty years old, far longer than most farm animals.

Cows have an excellent sense of smell and can detect odours from several kilometres away.
